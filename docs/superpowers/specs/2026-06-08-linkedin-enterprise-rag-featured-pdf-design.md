# LinkedIn Enterprise RAG Featured PDF Design

Date: 2026-06-08

## Purpose

Create a concise English PDF for Rosso Han's LinkedIn Featured section. It should
position Rosso as an engineering leader who builds trustworthy enterprise AI systems,
not as someone merely completing a course.

## Audience

- Recruiters hiring Applied AI engineering leaders
- Engineering and product executives evaluating enterprise AI experience
- Potential collaborators or customers interested in RAG and agent systems

## Format

- Six US Letter pages
- Clean editorial layout with dark navy, teal, and warm accent colors
- Short sections, diagrams, and evidence callouts rather than dense prose
- LinkedIn profile URL and QR code on the final page

## Narrative

1. Introduce the professional focus: trustworthy Enterprise RAG and AI agents.
2. Explain why ordinary retrieve-and-answer demos are insufficient.
3. Present the agent-first loop: intent, retrieve, decide, respond, trace, evaluate.
4. Show evaluation, governance, privacy, and architecture-escalation principles.
5. Separate demonstrated portfolio evidence from active experiments and next steps.

## Evidence Boundary

The PDF may reference verified portfolio evidence recorded in
`reviews/05-ai-career-positioning-and-linkedin.md`:

- a mixed-format RAG system with 487 indexed chunks
- a nine-node AI automation workflow
- 77 passing Avaloka AI tests as of June 8, 2026
- 200 passing Auralith tests as of June 8, 2026

Advanced retrieval methods such as GraphRAG, RAPTOR, and learned reranking must be
described as future experiments, not implemented production capabilities.
